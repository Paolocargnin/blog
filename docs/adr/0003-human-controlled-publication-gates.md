# Require human-controlled publication gates

No skill or AI agent may promote a Draft to Publication candidate or publish a candidate as a Post without explicit human approval. Every candidate must pass independent fact-checking and an adversarial counter-discussion before publication; AI output is assistance rather than evidence and factual claims attributed to AI must be verified against real sources. Counter-discussion produces a separate review instead of silently rewriting the candidate, and every finding must be fixed, rejected with rationale, or consciously accepted before publication.

Publication and meaning-changing Corrections are proposed through pull requests. The publishing workflow prepares a branch and summarizes the completed review evidence, then waits for the author to inspect the Cloudflare preview and explicitly merge.
