# Separate the evidence ledger from public Sources

Each reported article may maintain an exhaustive private `sources.md` ledger with source owner, URL or locator, access date, supported claims, and verification notes. Published Posts end with a curated `## Sources` section containing the public sources readers need to verify important claims; inline links may supplement it. Purely personal pieces may omit the section, but every publication review must explicitly propose its contents and record why omission is appropriate.
