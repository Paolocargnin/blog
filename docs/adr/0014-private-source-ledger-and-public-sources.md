# Separate the evidence ledger from public Sources

Each reported article may maintain an exhaustive private `sources.md` ledger with source owner, URL or locator, access date, supported claims, and verification notes. Published Posts may expose a curated `sources` frontmatter list containing the public sources readers need to verify important claims; the layout renders this as the public Sources section, while inline links may supplement it. Purely personal pieces may omit the list, but every publication review must explicitly propose its contents and record why omission is appropriate. The structured public-source format is defined by ADR 0017.
